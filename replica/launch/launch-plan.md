# Launch plan

1. Before launch: TestFlight beta with 10–20 friends; collect real feedback (with permission) — no invented proof.
2. Analytics + crash reporting: TelemetryDeck or Firebase Crashlytics (add to privacy labels).
3. Content: short-form "before/after reply" videos (TikTok/Reels) — the category grows on slideshows; lead with "sounds like you", never with fake screenshots of real people's chats.
4. Communities: r/Tinder, r/hingeapp, r/dating_advice — answer questions, don't spam; follow each sub's self-promo rules.
5. Product Hunt post leading with the fix: "an AI texting coach that refuses to write pickup-line spam."
6. First 10 users: talk to each by DM after day 3 — what reply did they send, did it work.
