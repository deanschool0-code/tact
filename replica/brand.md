## Palette

Dark ink base (#0D0F14), lime accent (#C8F25A). The category leans purple/pink gradients — lime is a different family.
Contrast: 17 pairs, 0 AA failures (`contrast.py replica/design/tokens.json`).

## Logo brief

- Idea: a speech bubble whose tail is a checkmark — "said right".
- Mark: symbol + lowercase wordmark "tact" (SF Pro Rounded Heavy).
- Works at 16px and as a 1024px iOS icon (no transparency): lime bubble-check on ink.
- Deliverables: SVG, 1024 icon, 1200x630 social image.
- Must not resemble any rizz app mark (no flame, no plug, no wing, no lips).
- A first-pass icon is generated in code: `ios/Tact/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon-1024.png` (from `tools/make_icon.py`).
